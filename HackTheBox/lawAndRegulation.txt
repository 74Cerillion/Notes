USA

----------------------------------------
Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)
----------------------------------------
    Federal law criminalizing computer access without authorization
    Indescript, not specific, and very controversial.
        As such, familiarization is vital
----------------------------------------
Digital Millunnium Copyright Act
----------------------------------------
    Provisions prohibiting circumventing tech measures protecting copyrighted works
    Includes clauses excepting valid ethical OffSec research activities
        Because of this, it's another important read
----------------------------------------
Electronic Communications Privacy Act
----------------------------------------
    Regulates incterception of E-Communications
    Consent laws regarding recording of electronic communications (disclosure when recording phone calls)
    Prohibits E-Comms in a court of law
    Rules governing what ISPs are allowed to do with their customer's ecomms
----------------------------------------
HIPAA
----------------------------------------
    You know this - PHI and PII
----------------------------------------
COPPA - Children's Online Privacy Protection Act
----------------------------------------
    Regulates collection of PI from children under 13
    OffSec researchers must take special steps to ensure that they're not collecting, using, or disclosing PI
        of a child under 13.
